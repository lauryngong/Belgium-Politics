## Leuven Experiences

### Phase 2 Contributions
For Phase II of the project, I contributed by refining and expanding our user personas and user stories to better match CRUD objectives while also making them more specific and realistic. I focused especially on the policymaker persona by adding agricultural demographic data, such as crop types, crop prices, and farm productivity, into the application design. I also created low-fidelity wireframes drawn on paper for multiple screens connected to my personas, helping visualize how users would interact with the platform. In addition, I helped design ER diagrams for each of the personas as well as contributed to the global ER diagram and relational model for the application database structure. These diagrams helped organize the relationships between datasets, users, reports, environmental conditions, and predictions within the system.

### What I found interesting
Outside of the project, I enjoyed listening to guest speakers involved with data and technology within the European Union government, especially hearing how large-scale data systems are used in policymaking and public infrastructure. I also spent time exploring Leuven and Brussels more throughout the week, and I went to a concert over the weekend, which was a fun experience outside of the academic program.
