## Leuven Experiences

### What I found interesting
One of the most interesting things I’ve learned so far during the Dialogue is how interconnected Europe is, both politically and technologically. Visiting the European Union buildings in Brussels made me realize how policies and collaboration between countries can impact millions of people across Europe. I’ve also enjoyed living in Leuven because the city combines historic culture with a strong student and technology-focused environment. In CS3200, I’ve found database design and data modeling especially interesting because they show how large amounts of information can be organized and used to solve real-world problems.

### Phase 1 Contributions
For the Phase 1 deliverable, my group explored different agriculture-related project ideas by considering the perspectives of multiple stakeholders before selecting our final topic. I specifically worked on creating the user persona for a policy maker stakeholder who would use agricultural and environmental data to make sustainability and land management decisions. I also helped research and select two datasets that could be used for the data models in our project.
