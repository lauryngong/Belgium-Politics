### Phase 3 Contributions
For Phase III, I primarily contributed to developing the policymaker section of the application. On the front end, I designed and implemented the Policy Maker Crop Map page and the Policy Maker Reports page in Streamlit based on our wireframes. These pages included interactive filters, map visualizations, report displays, summary statistics, and placeholder functionality for saving analyses and generating reports. Because our database schema and API routes are still evolving, the pages currently use mock data, but the overall structure and workflow have been established for future backend integration.
On the backend, I worked on the Comments and Reactions REST APIs. For comments, I implemented routes to list comments for a post, add a comment to a post, list comments by a user, retrieve a specific comment, update a comment, and delete a comment. For reactions, I implemented routes to list reactions for a post, add reactions (likes/dislikes), update reactions, and delete reactions. These routes support the community interaction features of the application and provide full CRUD functionality.

### What I found interesting
Outside of the project, our group was assigned to debrief and reflect on certain chosen guest speakers. One experience that stood out to me was our visit to Eurostat in Luxembourg. I found it especially interesting to hear from the guest speakers about how data is collected, standardized, and used across the European Union to support policymaking and decision-making. Learning about the challenges of managing data across many different countries and seeing how large-scale public data systems operate gave me a greater appreciation for the role of data in government. It was also interesting to compare some of the concepts discussed by the speakers with the goals of our own project, particularly how data can be organized and presented to help users make informed decisions.
