## First Week in Belgium!

From my first week on this dialogue, I have already learned so much. I have loved exploring the cities we live in and visit, hearing from guest speakers, visiting worldwide institutions, and learning in everyday classes. One thing that I have specifically found very interesting was when a teacher from KU Leuven came to talk to us about the EU before owe visitedParliament, and she told us about the voting rates in the EU. I was very surprised to hear how low the percentage of people who vote is, and how many EU citizens say that they do not even know how their own political system works. This week, I have also contributed to my team's Phase 1 deliverable by helping think of the idea and personas, writing the project description, and looking for datasets that we can use and helping load them in to make sure they are useable. 

Super excited for next week!
