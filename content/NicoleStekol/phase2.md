# Phase 2 Contributions
Phase 2 was definitely a lot of work but my group communicated and split it up for maximum progress and efficiency. I worked a lot with my fellow DS teammate Minju, but we split our tasks up so that she ended up working on the proof of concept KNN model for our agriculture dataset, and I worked on putting together a dataset for our model of predicting crop prices. 
As I discuss in the data handling section of the group blog post, I found, combined, and cleaned datasets on crop price, country, year, precipitation, and temperature. I was also in charge of the EDA section because the data we had for the prices was better to work with than the agriculture data. This means that I created multiple visualizations for the data, including a correlation heatmap for price and scatter plots for price versus temperature and precipitation. From these visualizations, I analyzed them for trends that will be used to help me create the linear regression model, where a farmer will input factors like their crop, country, precipitation, and temperature and will get a price they should sell that crop at. 
I have also began setting up the linear regression model by splitting the data into training and testing sets, but will continue this model after Phase 2.
