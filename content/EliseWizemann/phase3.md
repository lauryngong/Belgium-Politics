During this phase, I mainly worked on the database sections. I spent a lot of time tweaking certain aspects of my diagrams and SQL code to better fit the application and what my DS partners needed, especially since one of the models got completely overhauled and I needed to update the database to reflect this. I also helped Minju a lot with trying to fix her database, including finding the dataset she used for the new model. I also worked on a good portion of the REST api routes, including all of the routes user (see users by role and create new user) and for posts (create post, delete post, update post, see all posts and see posts by ID). In order to implement these APIs into the application, I updated the home page to have the drop down featuring the names by their user role, along with a basic discussion board on the farmer persona to CRUD discussion posts. I also worked a lot to help my teammates with their errors, especially since one of my rushed pushes caused a portion of these errors.

Europe is still pretty fun, I loved going to Ghent to see the lamb and visiting some new countries like France and Luxembourg. I definitely didn’t have a dull day at all, ranging from slightly concerning hotel rooms to constantly running to train stations, and trying to entertain myself in a small mall for over two hours.
