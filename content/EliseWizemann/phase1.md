Having never really travelled much out of the country (or in general), I’ve been looking forward to this Dialogue ever since I got in. Being in an entirely new continent has been so much more fun than I could imagine, and I’ve loved being able to see so many new things ranging from EU institutions to Brussels, historic buildings casually scattered around, and the Leuven Hospital. I’ve especially enjoyed the workshops where we learn about how data is handled and secured in the EU, and the balance between data privacy and our responsibility to protect children online.

Despite my adventures I still have dedicated a good amount of time to this project so far, and I have worked on brainstorming our ideas and models, creating the Carrie persona, and looking for datasets. I've also worked a lot on the layout and theming of the website (I am NOT a UI designer, sorry).
