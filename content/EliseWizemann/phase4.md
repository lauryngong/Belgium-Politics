During our final phase, a lot of the work I did was updated hardcoded pages to be interactive. I worked (and then reworked based on feedback) the page for farmers to add their farm, their farm location, and their crop records. Most of the rest of my work was focused on the researcher persona, where I worked on the map view with its filters, the export data, and researcher condition pages specifically. Our mock data was also lacking a bit, so I generated most of the mock data for the users, farms, and user_growing_data tables I also worked to update some of our previous blog posts based on the feedback given. This was mainly blurry screenshots in phase 3, along with fixing the REST API matrix.

This last phase also had us working together as a group much more than the previous phases, so much of all of our work was helping each other out. I talked a lot with my teammates on their opinions of the database and what tables they are and are not using, and then updated a lot of the database based on this. I initially thought that having a farm_location table be a weak entity to a farm so it can have multiple locations was a good idea, but as we started to implement our REST APIs and think about it more we found this to be more and more impractical and unhelpful, so we agreed to combine it to one table. We also would all help each other as needed, so I would help out my teammates on less crucial tasks like fixing spelling and formatting, adding a new logo, and testing what they wrote.

Looking back on this dialogue, I am very greatful for being able to have this experience. I feel like I've learned a lot academically through my class and the guest speakers we had. Being exposed to this "European Mindset" of regulation has definitely changed my way of thinking, since I can see the appeal of wanting to regulate something new as much as possible, but I also now understand the drawbacks to this approach. I feel like I have learned a lot about myself through this too, such as my ability to adapt to new situations and how I handle stressful situations abroad without my family to support me in the moment. I feel that I have become much more independent as a person, and I will always be thankful and fondly look back on this experience and all the memories I made through it.
