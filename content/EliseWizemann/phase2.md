For this phase, I focused a lot on trying to expand upon our topic idea. Based on the feedback we got we knew that our application was missing something, so I spent a lot of time thinking of different features to add until we decided on the complaint board. Throughout the week we also had a lot of discussions about what we expected our application to do, and these helped to clear up the scope and features of our project. Through these I also updated some of the personas to follow more of the CRUD principles and better integrate the new features we decided to add.

    
I also worked a lot on the database modeling, both the conceptual layout and then all the diagrams and the initial DDL pass. I definitely spent an absurd amount of time on this, mainly due to how much our application changed over this time. A part of the database modelling also relied on knowing what data columns our machine learning models would be using since the users would be inputting this information, so some of the tables (mainly idealCropData) were constantly being updated. The ER diagrams were very helpful for this, as they could pretty easily be updated and tweaked. Once we got a sort of finalized ER diagram I then made the Relational Model, and created a DDL based on that. There are a few inconsistencies between the two, so in the future I will definitely talk about these with my group and professors to make sure we all have a common understanding and solution on how to fix this.


Beyond the project, I really have enjoyed visiting different cities. I loved seeing the Ghent Altarpiece, Bruges was very pretty and a bit cooler, and as always Brussels is nice to see.
